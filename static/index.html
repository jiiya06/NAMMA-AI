      What NammaAI cannot do

      • No live data: no delays, crowding or next-train times.
      • No booking or payments. Bengaluru metro only.
      • The Pink and Blue lines are not open yet, so they are not in the route finder.

      Reading a route card

      Times and distances are estimates. Fares are approximate and follow the BMRCL slab rule. The date on the card is when our data was last checked. Give a thumbs down if an answer looks wrong.

      Privacy

      Messages are sent to the AI provider shown at the bottom of the chat and saved in a local database with timestamps. Phone numbers, emails and ID numbers are removed before saving. Please do not share personal details.

      Shortcuts

      Enter sends. Shift+Enter adds a new line. Press / to jump to the message box.

      Data sources

      Metro lines and station order from BMRCL (english.bmrc.co.in), checked October 2026. Fare rules from BMRCL announcements reported by Deccan Herald and trayaan.com. Project: BCS515B, Phase 1.
