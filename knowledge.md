## Network status (29 Oct 2026)
- Operating metro lines: Purple (Whitefield to Challaghatta), Green (Madavara to Silk Institute) and Yellow (RV Road to Bommasandra).
- Interchanges: Majestic (Purple and Green) and RV Road (Green and Yellow).
- Pink Line: the 7.5 km elevated section from Kalena Agrahara to Tavarekere is NOT open yet. It was awaiting the Commissioner of Metro Railway Safety's clearance in October 2026. The underground part to Nagawara is expected in 2027.
- Blue Line (Outer Ring Road, KR Puram to HSR Layout, and airport link): not open. Trial runs were planned from late 2026; opening is targeted around May 2027 for the ORR section and December 2027 for the airport section.
- Source: news reports from Swarajya (15 Oct 2026), Business Today (10 Aug 2026), Curly Tales (23 Oct 2026). Accessed 29 Oct 2026. Official status is on english.bmrc.co.in.

## Metro fares
- BMRCL charges by the number of stations travelled. For fare purposes each station counts as 1 km, whatever the real distance.
- Approximate slabs (2025 structure): up to 2 km Rs 10; 2 to 4 km Rs 20; 4 to 6 km Rs 30; 6 to 8 km Rs 40; 8 to 10 km Rs 50; 10 to 15 km Rs 60; 15 to 20 km Rs 70; 20 to 25 km Rs 80; above 25 km Rs 90 (the maximum).
- A 5 percent annual revision planned for 9 Feb 2026 was put on hold by BMRCL on 8 Feb 2026. VERIFY whether any later revision applies.
- Source: BMRCL announcements via trayaan.com (Feb 2025, Feb 2026), Deccan Herald, ThePrint. Accessed 29 Oct 2026.

## Timings (VERIFY)
- Trains usually start early morning and stop late evening, with a later start on Sundays. Exact first and last train times differ by station and direction. The bot does not have them, so it must send users to the BMRCL website or app.

## Tickets and cards (VERIFY)
- Passengers can use a Namma Metro smart card, which gives a small discount on fares, or buy a ticket at the station counter or through the QR ticket options. Check the BMRCL site for current card discount and top-up rules.

## Luggage, rules and etiquette (VERIFY)
- Stations have security checks. Large or unusual items and prohibited items are decided by BMRCL; the bot must not guess the list and should point to the BMRCL website.
- Give priority seats to elderly, disabled and pregnant passengers, and stay behind the yellow line on the platform.

## Accessibility (VERIFY)
- Stations generally have lifts, ramps and tactile paths. Exact facilities vary by station. Ask station staff.

## Airport
- The metro does not reach Kempegowda International Airport yet. The Blue Line airport section is targeted for December 2027. Until then, BMTC airport buses (called Vayu Vajra) are the public transport option. VERIFY routes on the BMTC website.

## BMTC buses
- NammaAI has BMTC route and stop data from a community-maintained copy of the BMTC schedule (Vonter/bmtc-gtfs on GitHub, feed dated 7 Sep 2026, accessed 29 Oct 2026). The data can have wrong timetables, missing routes and routes that no longer run.
- Bus times and fares are estimates. There is no live bus tracking. For live buses use the official BMTC website or the Namma BMTC app.
- Karnataka's Shakti scheme gives eligible women free travel on ordinary BMTC buses. It does not apply to the metro. VERIFY current terms.

## Safety and help
- For emergencies, harassment or lost items, tell the user to contact station staff or the official BMRCL or BMTC helpline from their websites, or call 112 in an emergency.
- NammaAI never asks for passwords, OTPs, payment details or personal information.
